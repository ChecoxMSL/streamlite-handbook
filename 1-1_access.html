📖 QA Agent Guide

Welcome to the QA Bonus Grading App. This guide covers everything you need to audit sales agents' weekly performance — from logging in to marking your last agent purple.

🔒 Your Scope of Work: Your responsibility is to complete the grading of all agents assigned to you in the sidebar. Once every agent shows Purple 🟣, notify the Administrator for final processing. You do not manage Drive uploads, HR sync, or Chat dispatch — those are Admin-only steps.

1. Access & Initial Setup

1.1 Login & Authentication

Navigate to the app URL provided by your Administrator. Enter your Username and Password on the Login tab. If you are a first-time user, switch to the Register tab to create your credentials.

💡 Tip: Forgot your PIN? Use the Forgot PIN tab on the login screen. All login activity is logged in the system — contact the Administrator if you experience access issues.

1.2 🚦 Audit Progress Semaphores

Each agent in the sidebar shows a combination of a type icon + a progress color. Reading these correctly lets you track the full lifecycle of an audit at a glance.

📈 Progress Color — What has been done

Icon Meaning
⚪Pending — not yet graded this week
🟣Graded — PDF generated & uploaded to Drive
🔵Synced — HR Spreadsheet updated (Admin)
🟢Sent — dispatched via Google Chat (Admin) 🏁

👤 Agent Type — Independent of progress

These icons appear regardless of the progress color.

Icon Meaning
⭐Full Bonus — Whitelist or SP Comercial Tier (45+ leads)
🚫Suspended — Automatic disqualification from bonus
