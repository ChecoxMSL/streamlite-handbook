QA Agent Guide

Your responsibility is to complete the grading of all agents assigned to you in the sidebar. Once every agent shows Purple 🟣, notify the Administrator for final processing. You do not manage Drive uploads, HR sync, or Chat dispatch — those are Admin-only steps covered in Chapter 02.
