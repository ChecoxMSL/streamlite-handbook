Admin Guide

For QA Agents with Administrator access. This chapter covers environment management, penalty configuration, the two-stage dispatch process, and corrective actions.

Admin Scope of Work

As Administrator, you are responsible for the steps that happen after QA agents finish grading. Once all agents show Purple 🟣, you complete the pipeline: configure rules, sync to HR (🔵), and dispatch via Google Chat (🟢). You also manage the Whitelist, SP Tier, environment settings, and corrective actions.
