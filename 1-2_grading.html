✅ The Grading Workflow

This page walks you through every step from selecting an agent to confirming the grade is locked in. Follow this flow exactly — in the correct order — to avoid incomplete records.

2.1 The Sidebar — Your Mission Control

The sidebar gives you a real-time view of this week's audit progress. Before you start any session, scan the sidebar to identify who still has a white label ⚪.

  • ⚪ White label — Pending. This agent has not been graded yet this week. This is your target.
  • 🟣 Purple label — Done. You have already completed the audit and the PDF is saved.
⚠️ Important: Only agents with a ⚪ white label are available for grading in the current week. If you accidentally click a purple one, the system will open a read-only view — you won't be able to overwrite it unless the Administrator initiates a Re-grade.

Your goal every shift: Ensure every agent in your assigned list turns Purple 🟣 before you log off.

2.2 Step-by-Step Grading

  1. Select the Agent.

    Click on any agent showing a white ⚪ label in the sidebar (e.g., ⚪ sp mathew). The main panel will load their profile and call log dashboard.

  2. Select Dispositions & Review Call Logs.

    Choose which call dispositions you want to audit. The system will automatically surface 5 call logs per disposition pulled from BigQuery for the configured date range.

    💡 Pro Tip — Refresh Button: If the 5 calls shown don't give you a representative sample, click the Refresh button underneath that disposition block. The system will query BigQuery for a fresh set of 5 logs. You can refresh as many times as needed.
  3. Fill in the Grading Tab.

    Switch to the Grading & Score tab. For each infraction category, enter the count of flagged calls you found. The system compares your counts against the penalty thresholds defined by the Administrator and auto-calculates the deductions.

    ⚖️ Default Penalty Reference

    Penalty rules are set by the Administrator and may be updated at any time. What you see in-app is always the live version.

    Infraction Deduction Triggers when…
    Call Avoidance−1.0 ★≥ 1 flagged call
    Wrong Disposition−1.0 ★≥ 3 flagged calls
    Script Violation−1.0 ★≥ 3 flagged calls
    Tone / Attitude−1.0 ★≥ 1 flagged call
    Other−1.0 ★≥ 1 flagged call
    💡 How Penalties Work: The system loads thresholds from Admin_Config on the Sheets backend. For each infraction, if your flagged count meets or exceeds the threshold, the star deduction is applied automatically. Below the threshold = no penalty for that category.
  4. Add Qualitative Feedback.

    Fill in the written observations for the agent. This text appears directly on the generated PDF. Be specific and constructive — agents will read this.

  5. Click Generate.

    The system will:

    • Calculate the final star rating based on your inputs and any penalties.
    • Select the correct output format automatically (Report Card, Certificate, or Suspension Banner).
    • Generate the PDF/PNG and upload it to the correct Google Drive folder.
    • Save the record to Audit_Ledger in the Sheets backend.
    • Immediately update the agent's sidebar label from ⚪ White → 🟣 Purple.
    ✅ You're Done! Once you see the Purple label, your job for that agent is complete. The Administrator will handle the remaining steps (🔵 HR Sync → 🟢 Chat dispatch).
