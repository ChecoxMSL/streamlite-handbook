📁 Drive & Handoff Procedure

All files generated by the app are uploaded automatically. Your only job is to confirm they land in the right place if needed — and to notify the Administrator once every agent is purple.

3. Document Management

3.1 Automatic File Routing — How It Works

When you click Generate, services/drive.py automatically routes the file to the correct Google Drive location based on:

  • The agent's name prefix — determines the office subfolder.
  • The current week's date range — always Monday to Sunday, regardless of when during the week the PDF is generated.

3.2 Week Slot Format

Each audit is permanently tied to a week slot in the format MM/DD–MM/DD. For example:

  • 03/09–03/15 — Last Week
  • 03/16–03/22 — Current Week

When a new week starts, the app automatically archives the previous week's records to Audit History. You can browse past weeks using the 📚 History tab inside the app — it's read-only and safe to explore.

4. Handoff Procedure

4.1 Your Completion Checklist

Before you notify the Administrator, run through this quick check:

  • 🟣 Every agent in your sidebar shows Purple. Scroll through the full list — don't miss anyone near the bottom.
  • 📄 Verify files in Drive (optional but recommended). Spot-check 1–2 agents to confirm their PDFs appear in the correct office subfolder.
  • 💬 Notify the Administrator. Message them directly — via Google Chat or whatever channel your team uses — to confirm the sidebar is fully purple.

✅ 4.2 What Happens After You Notify

Once you hand off, the Administrator takes over and completes the two remaining steps that are outside your scope:

  1. 🔵 HR Sync — The Audit Ledger data is pushed to the HR Spreadsheet. Agent labels turn Blue.
  2. 🟢 Chat Dispatch — Individual bonus results are sent to each agent via Google Chat DM. Agent labels turn Green 🏁.

You will see the sidebar colors update in real time as the Administrator runs each step — no action required on your end.

⚠️ Do Not Re-grade Without Authorization. If you realize you made an error after generating a purple label, do not attempt to grade the same agent again. Contact the Administrator — they have access to the Re-grade function in Admin Settings.
